# Troubleshooting Method (the standard 6 steps, also tested on CompTIA A+)

1. **Identify the problem.** Ask the user questions, look for recent changes, and back up data first.
2. **Establish a theory** of probable cause (question the obvious).
3. **Test the theory.** If it's wrong, go back to step 2 or escalate.
4. **Make a plan** to fix it and carry it out.
5. **Verify** the system works, and put preventive measures in place.
6. **Document** what you found, what you did, and the result.
