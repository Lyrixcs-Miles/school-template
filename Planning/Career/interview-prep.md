# Interview Prep

Type `star` + Tab to write a behavioural answer.

## Technical
- **Coding problems:** see Computer Science Core/Data Structures and Algorithms/Exercises/practice-problems.md
- **Explain out loud** while you solve. Interviewers want to hear how you think.
- Know Big-O for common operations (array access, hash map lookup, sorting)
- Be ready to talk through any project on your CV in detail

## Common questions: write your answers below
### About you
- **Tell me about yourself** (60–90 seconds: background → what you've learned → what you want)
- **Why this company or role?**
- **What are you learning right now?**

### Behavioural (answer with STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- A time you solved a difficult problem
- A time you made a mistake and what you learned
- A time you worked in a team, or disagreed with someone
- A project you're proud of

### IT, networking, and security roles
- What happens when you type a URL into a browser?
- What's the difference between TCP and UDP?
- How would you troubleshoot "the internet isn't working"?
- What's the difference between authentication and authorisation?

## Questions to ask them
- What does a typical day look like in this role?
- How do you support new people learning on the job?
- What would success look like in the first 3 months?
